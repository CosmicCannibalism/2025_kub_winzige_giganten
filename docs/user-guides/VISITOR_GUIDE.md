# 📱 Visitor Guide - Connect to the Exhibition

**Print this for your visitors!**

---

## How to Experience the Exhibition on Your Device

### Step 1: Connect to WiFi

1. Open **WiFi Settings** on your device
2. Look for network: **`winzige_giganten`**
3. Tap to connect
4. Enter password: **`winzigegiganten`**

*Don't worry about the "No Internet" message - this is normal!*

---

### Step 2: Open the Exhibition

Your device should automatically open the exhibition page.

**If it doesn't:**
1. Open your web browser (Safari/Chrome)
2. Type: **`192.168.4.1`**
3. Press Go

---

### Step 3: Install on Your Homescreen (Optional)

**For iPhone/iPad:**
1. Tap the **Share button** (box with arrow ↑)
2. Scroll down
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Tap **"Add"**

**For Android:**
1. Tap the **Menu** (three dots)
2. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
3. Tap **"Add"**

---

### Step 4: Enjoy!

- Watch videos
- Explore the exhibition
- Works offline after initial load

---

## 💡 Tips

- **Videos take a moment to load** - please be patient
- **Keep your device connected to WiFi** for best experience
- **Battery**: The exhibition uses minimal battery power
- **Questions?** Ask our staff for help

---

## 🚫 No Internet?

This exhibition runs on a **local network** without internet. This is intentional and allows the exhibition to work anywhere without requiring an internet connection.
